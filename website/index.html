Your TV. Your way.

A home screen you can make your own.
Down to the smallest detail.

Place it your way

Move the clock, weather and shortcuts. Set safe-area padding, snap to edges and align your top row.

Make your apps your own

Choose minimal pills or colorful cards. Reorder apps, resize icons, adjust spacing and hide labels.

Bring your screen to life

Use your photos and videos as the backdrop. Set a slideshow, choose the fit and control playback.

Weather, in your style

Choose Minimal, Compact, Scenic or just an icon and temperature. Resize the content and go portrait.

Give navigation a sound

Choose from five navigation sounds, preview them and set the volume. Or keep things quiet.

Keep each TV personal

Save TVs in the companion and switch between them. Each keeps its own layout and preferences.
