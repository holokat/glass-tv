# Assets

The repository includes the Glass TV app artwork, generated wallpaper and weather artwork, generated non-Sony TV preview frames, shortcut marks with their source attribution, and the CC0 navigation-sound notice.

The raw Central interface-icon collection is not included. `CENTRAL_ICONS.md` and `shared/icons-manifest.json` identify the local licensed-icon prerequisite for source builds.

Generated-art provenance has been reduced to remove local filesystem history. The supplied Sony frame is not included in the release assets.
